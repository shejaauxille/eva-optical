Our Story

Vision Elevated
to an Art

Eva Optical is Rwanda's trusted name in premium eye care — a destination where precision optical science meets refined craftsmanship. From Kigali to Gisenyi to Musanze, we bring world-class vision care to every corner of the country.

Our mission: to provide reliable, affordable, and professional optical solutions, helping every client see better and feel more confident — through quality lenses, frames, and expert consultations.

Our values: integrity, care, innovation, and excellence guide every service we provide. We treat our clients like family and ensure they receive expert attention every time.
